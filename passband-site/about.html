Passband

About Passband

An email client for Mac and iOS that triages your Gmail.

Passband is two pieces. squelchd is a daemon that connects to your Gmail mailbox over the Gmail API, syncs your messages, and scores each one so that the mail worth your attention is separated from the mail that is not. Passband is the app you read it in, on macOS and iOS.

The name is the point. A squelch mutes everything below a threshold; the passband is the band a filter lets through. The inbox holds what made it through, and everything else is filed without asking you first.

What it does

  • Triage. Every incoming message is scored and sorted into a tier, using rules you write in plain language plus an importance model. Newsletters, receipts, and notifications are filed automatically; mail from people you correspond with is always let through.
  • Read. Threads are rendered oldest to newest, with inline images and attachments, full-text search across your mailbox, and Quick Look preview for anything attached.
  • Act. Archive, label, mark read or unread, and park a thread until a time you name so it comes back when it is relevant.
  • Write. Compose, reply, and forward with Markdown formatting, contact autocomplete, and attachments, sent from your own Gmail address.
  • Extract. Deadlines, verification codes, and package tracking are pulled out of your mail and surfaced as cards, so you do not have to reopen a message to find the one line that mattered.
  • Ask. A built-in assistant can search and summarize your mailbox, and drafts any action for your confirmation before it touches anything.

How it uses your Google account

Passband connects to Gmail through the Gmail API with your consent, over OAuth. It requests these permissions and no others:

PermissionWhy Passband needs it
gmail.readonly Read your messages so they can be triaged, threaded, searched, and displayed, and so deadlines and tracking numbers can be extracted.
gmail.modify Apply what triage or you decided: archive a message, add or remove a label, mark it read or unread, bring a parked thread back.
gmail.send Send the mail you write in Passband: replies, forwards, new messages, and invitations you choose to share.

Read access and write access are held as separate credentials, and the sync path is structurally unable to load the one that can write. What is stored, where it lives, and who can reach it is set out in full in the Privacy Policy.

Two ways to run it

Self-host. Run the daemon on your own machine, NAS, or server. Your mail and your Google credentials stay on your disk and never reach infrastructure we operate. The software is open source under the MIT license, and the setup is written up on the self-host page.

Hosted. We run the daemon for you, one isolated instance per account, which is what makes always-on triage and mobile push possible. Your mail and your Google refresh token are stored on our servers; the Privacy Policy explains exactly what that means. Hosted accounts are invitation-only while the product is in preview.
